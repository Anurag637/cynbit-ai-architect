### Strategic Selection: Revio 2.0
Cynbit operates three proprietary software products:
1. **TenantEase:** Property & Tenant Management
2. **Cynbit Studio:** Career Intelligence & Code Lab (tied to BitElevate)
3. **Revio:** AI Review & Reputation Hub

While simple review notifications are commoditized, enterprise multi-location brands (hotels, QSRs, diagnostic clinics) face existential vulnerability from **unhandled negative reviews, food-safety crises, and viral legal allegations**.

**Revio 2.0** transforms Revio from a reactive notification widget into an **AI-Powered Operational Brand Intelligence Suite**:

1. **Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA):** Pinpoints the exact operational department causing friction (*Food Quality, Service Speed, Staff Courtesy, Cleanliness, Billing, Pricing*).
2. **Heuristic Crisis Screening (0–100):** Flags food poisoning, fraud, discrimination, and legal threats for immediate `< 15-minute` containment.
3. **Persona-Calibrated Contextual Responder:** Generates brand-aligned replies with automated ticket generation (`#SIM-XXXX`) and legal safety guardrails.
4. **Multi-Location Analytics:** Uncovers branch-specific performance bottlenecks across regional locations.
